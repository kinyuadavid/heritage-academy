How the school is arranged

Three blocks, four stages, one gate.

The little ones have their own block, their own play yard and their own assembly, so a three-year-old is never lost among fifteen-year-olds.

The early years block

Playgroup, PP1 and PP2 sit apart from the rest of the school, with a fenced yard, a nap room and two teachers in every room. Parents drop off and collect at their own gate.

The primary block

Grade 1 to Grade 6, one class teacher per class who stays with them for the year, and specialist teachers for Kiswahili, science and creative arts from Grade 4.

The junior school block

Grade 7 to Grade 9, with subject teachers, three laboratories and a computer room. KJSEA is sat at the end of Grade 9, and we sit down with parents about senior school a full year before.

Support

Nobody is left to sink quietly.

Marks are entered after every test, and a learner who drops two grades in a subject is picked up the same week, not at the end of term.

  • A remedial hour every Tuesday and Thursday, by subject, not by class
  • A phone call home the week a grade slips, from the class teacher
  • Senior school guidance from Grade 8, with parents in the room
  • A quiet room for learners who need one, and two trained counsellors
