School life

The hours between the lessons.

A fenced play yard for the little ones, two boarding houses from Grade 4, and twenty clubs that meet whether or not anyone is watching.

Boarding, from Grade 4

Two houses, a matron in each.

Boarding starts at Grade 4, never before. Boarders sleep eight to twelve in a room, with a locker each and a reading table under the window. The two houses compete at everything, all year, and take it far too seriously.

  • A matron resident in every house, and a duty teacher every night
  • Lights out at 21:00, phones kept in the house office
  • Laundry twice a week, done by the school
  • Visiting on the last Sunday of the month, 10am to 4pm
  • A sick bay with a nurse on call, and Wajir County Referral ten minutes away

Beyond the classroom

Twenty clubs, and every one of them meets.

Games run Monday to Thursday from 16:20, and clubs take Friday afternoon. Everyone belongs to at least one.

Sport

Around the campus

Eleven hectares, and every one of them used.

Food

Three meals, cooked on site.

Breakfast is porridge or tea with bread. Lunch and supper rotate through rice, ugali, githeri, beans, greens and meat twice a week, with fruit on Sunday. The menu is pinned outside the dining hall and the head of house eats what the learners eat.

Learners with a medical diet are catered for; tell us at admission and the matron and the cook both get a copy.

Lunch, 13:10

What a boarder brings

  • Two sets of uniform and a games kit (bought at school)
  • Bedding pack, or buy ours at admission
  • A padlock, a torch and a water bottle
  • Toiletries for the term
  • No phones, no jewellery, no food in the dormitory
