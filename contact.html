Finding the school

From Wajir town.

  1. Take the Griftu road

    Head west out of town past the county referral hospital.

  2. Three kilometres on

    Pass the Makoror turning; the school wall starts on your right.

  3. The green and cream gate

    You cannot miss it. Report to the gate office and they will call the duty teacher.
