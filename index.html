Why families choose us

Big enough to offer everything. Small enough to know your child.

We opened in 2009 with 38 learners in two classrooms. The promise then is the promise now: no child disappears at the back of the room.

  • 1

    Thirteen learners to a teacher

    Classes are capped at 30, and two teachers stay in every pre-primary room. Each child has a teacher who answers for them by name at every staff meeting.

  • 2

    One school, from three years old to fifteen

    Playgroup to Grade 9 on one campus, so a child moves up without changing school, and the teachers already know them.

  • 3

    A parent report every three weeks

    Marks, attendance and a written note from the class teacher, on your phone, not only at the end of term.

From the principal

“We measure ourselves by the child at the back of the room.”

Any school can show you its best learner. The question I ask my staff is a harder one: what happened this term to the child who was struggling in March? That is the school you are choosing.

We are not the biggest school in Wajir and we do not intend to be. We are the one where a teacher notices on the Tuesday, not at the end of term, and where a parent gets a phone call rather than a surprise.

Abdirahman SheikhPrincipal since 2016

Where our leavers go

Ninety-six out of a hundred.

That is the share of our Grade 9 leavers who were placed in their first-choice senior school last year, up from eighty-eight in 2023. The work behind it is unglamorous: marked books, evening prep, and a call home when something slips.
